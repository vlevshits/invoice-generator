Invoice Generator

A fast, local desktop application built for freelancers, independent contractors, and business entities to effortlessly build professional PDF invoices, manage client counterparties, and automatically back up ledger data to Google Drive.

About Invoice Generator

Invoice Generator simplifies professional billing for businesses worldwide. Built as a high-performance desktop software using Tauri, React, and Typst, it stores all customer records and transaction data 100% locally on your computer.

⚡ Professional PDF Export

Instantly generate vector-quality PDF invoices with multi-currency support (GEL, EUR, USD, GBP) powered by the Typst typesetting engine.

📂 Client & Bank Management

Organize client counterparty details, tax IDs, legal addresses, and multiple seller bank accounts with ease.

☁️ Google Drive Backup

Optional automated cloud backup that syncs generated PDF invoices and maintains an aggregated ledger summary spreadsheet in your Google Drive.

Google Drive Integration & Purpose

Invoice Generator includes an optional Google Drive cloud sync feature. When enabled by the user, the application connects to your Google Drive to keep your billing records safely backed up.

Google OAuth Scope Usage (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file):

Invoice Generator uses the restricted per-file access scope. This grants the application permission ONLY to access, create, or update files and folders that were explicitly created by Invoice Generator (specifically inside your custom "Invoice Generator" folder).

The application CANNOT access, read, edit, or delete any other files, photos, or documents in your Google Drive account.

How Google User Data is Used:

  • Automated PDF Storage: Uploads newly generated invoice PDFs to your specified Google Drive folder.
  • In-Place Updates: Replaces or updates existing invoice PDFs when changes occur, preventing duplicate file creation.
  • Ledger Spreadsheet Sync: Creates and maintains an Invoices Ledger Summary Google Sheet with invoice totals, dates, and payment statuses.
  • Zero Data Transmission: OAuth authentication tokens are stored locally on your device and are never sent to external servers or third-party tracking services.

Privacy Policy & Data Security

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

At Invoice Generator, we prioritize user data privacy and security:

  • Local Database: All company profiles, client counterparties, bank information, and invoice records are stored in a local SQLite database (invoices.db) on your local hard drive.
  • No External Server Tracking: We do not run external backend database servers, telemetry tracking, or user analytics.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance: Invoice Generator's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking Access: You can disconnect Google Drive at any time inside the app settings by clicking Disconnect, or revoke permissions directly via your Google Account Permissions Settings.
