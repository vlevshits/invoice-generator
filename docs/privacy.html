Desktop Application Privacy Notice

Privacy Policy & Google API Disclosure

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

Invoice Generator is a privacy-first, local desktop application built for creating, managing, and exporting professional PDF invoices.

1. Data Collection & Local Storage

We respect your privacy. Invoice Generator operates 100% locally on your machine. All company profiles, client counterparties, bank details, and invoice transactions are stored exclusively in an encrypted or local SQLite database on your personal computer.

  • We do NOT operate central database servers.
  • We do NOT track, collect, transmit, or share your financial data or customer details.

2. Google Drive Integration (`drive.file`)

Invoice Generator offers an optional Google Drive integration that allows you to automatically back up and synchronize generated PDF invoices and summary spreadsheets to your personal Google Drive.

Limited Scope Access: Our application uses the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file OAuth scope. This permission is per-file access and allows the app ONLY to access, create, or update files and folders that were explicitly created by or opened with Invoice Generator (e.g., inside your designated "Invoice Generator" folder).

Invoice Generator CANNOT view, modify, read, or delete any of your existing private files, photos, or documents on Google Drive.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Invoice Generator's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. How We Use Google User Data

  • File Creation & Updates: To export generated Typst PDF invoices and update an aggregated Invoices Ledger Summary Google Sheet in your designated Drive folder.
  • In-Place Synchronization: To overwrite or update previously exported invoice PDFs in-place without creating duplicate files.
  • No Data Sharing: Your Google OAuth access tokens are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to third parties or remote analytics services.

5. Disconnecting & Revoking Access

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time inside the app settings by clicking Disconnect Drive. You may also revoke access at any time via your Google Account Security Settings.

6. Contact & Open Source Support

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or app security, please contact us via our GitHub Repository or support channel.
